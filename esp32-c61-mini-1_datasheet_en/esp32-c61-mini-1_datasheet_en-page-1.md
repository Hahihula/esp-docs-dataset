**Title:**
ESP32-C61-MINI-1  
ESP32-C61-MINI-1U

**Subtitle:**
Datasheet Version 0.6

**Body Text:**
Module that supports 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi (802.11ax) and Bluetooth® 5 (LE)

Built around ESP32-C61 series of SoCs, 32-bit RISC-V single-core microprocessor  
Flash up to 8 MB, PSRAM up to 8 MB  
UP TO 23 GPIOs, rich set of peripherals  
On-board PCB antenna or external antenna connector
