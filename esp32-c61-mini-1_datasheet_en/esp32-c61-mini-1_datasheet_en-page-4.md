#### Table 2: ESP32-C61-MINI-1U (CONN) Series Comparison

| Ordering Code | Flash^2,3 | PSRAM | Embedded Chip | Ambient Temp. ^5 | Size^5 |

^2 By default, the SPI flash on the module operates at a maximum clock frequency of 80 MHz and does not support the auto suspend feature. If you need the flash to automatically suspend features please contact us.

^3 The flash is integrated in the chip's package.
^4 Ambient temperature specifies the recommended temperature range for operation immediately outside the Espressif module.
^5 The thickness (2.4 mm) of ESP32-C61-MINI-1-N8R8 and ESP32-C61-MINI-1U-N8R8 given in this section is preliminary and may be fine-tuned during final release.

^6 ESP32-C61-MINI-1U uses ANT1 by default. To order the ANT2 module, add T2 to general ordering code for example: ESP32-C61-MINI-1-U-N8R7T2

At the core of these modules is ESP32-C61, a 32-bit RISC-V single-core processor.
