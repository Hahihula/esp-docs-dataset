**Title:**
10 Physical Dimensions

**Subtitle:**
10.2 Dimensions of External Antenna Connector

**Body Text:**
ESP32-C61-MINI-1U uses the third generation external antenna connector as shown in Figure 13 Dimensions of External Antenna Connector. This connector is compatible with the following connectors:

- W.FL Series connector from Hirose
- MHF III connector from I-PHIX
- AMMC connector from Amphenol

**Figure Caption:**
Figure 13: Dimensions of External Antenna Connector

**Diagram Annotations (from left to right, top to bottom):**

1. **Top Left Diagram:** 
   - Measurements:
     - Diameter labeled as "A" is approximately 20mm.
     - Height from the base circle centerline up and down appears around ±5 mm.

2. **Middle Top Diagram:**
   - Labeled components include "CONTACT," with dimensions indicating a height of about 17 mm, including ground contact below it which measures roughly another ~4-6 mm in length vertically.
   
3. **Top Right Diagram:** 
   - Shows the side view and top-down views:
     - Dimensions indicate an overall diameter around ±20mm (with tolerance).
     - The height from base to peak is approximately 17 mm.

**Additional Details:**
- Unit of measurement for all dimensions provided in millimeters.
- Tolerance specified as +/−0.1 mm
- Housing material noted as thermoplastic, white UL94V-0; contact and shell materials are copper alloy gold plated throughout the connector assembly

**Performance Specifications (at bottom):**
- Contact resistance: 20mΩHM Max.
- Dielectric withstanding voltage at AC for a minute is specified to be up to ±500VAC
- Insulation Resistance minimum of 500MOHM.
