**Section Title:**
3.1 Pin Layout

**Body Text:**
The pin diagram below shows the approximate location of pins on the module. For the actual diagram drawn to scale, please refer to Figure 10.1 Module Dimensions.

**Image Description (Figure):**
- Caption: "Figure 3-1. Pin Layout (Top View)"
- The image is a schematic layout showing various pin positions and labels such as GND for ground pins, NC indicating no connection or unused space, IOx where x represents different IO pins like IO0 to IO9.

**Note A:**
The zone marked with dotted lines is the antenna keepout zone. The pin diagram is applicable to ESP32-C3-MINI-1 and ESP32-C3-MINI-1U, but the latter has no antenna keepout zone.
To learn more about the keepout zone for module’s antenna on the base board, please refer to
ESP32-C3 Hardware Design Guidelines > Section General Principles of PCB Layout for Modules.
