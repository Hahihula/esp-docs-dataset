10.2 Dimensions of External Antenna Connector

**Body Text:**
ESP32-C3-MINI-1U uses the third generation external antenna connector as shown in Figure 10-3 Dimensions of External Antenna Connector. This connector is compatible with the following connectors:
- W.FL Series connector from Hirose
- MHF III connector from I-PHIX
- AMC connector from Amphenol
