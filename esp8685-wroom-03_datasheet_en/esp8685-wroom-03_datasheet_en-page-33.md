### Subtitle: Module Placement for PCB Design

If module-on-board design is adopted, attention should be paid while positioning the module on the base board to minimize interference of the baseboard with antenna performance.
  
For details about module placement refer to [ESP32-C3 Hardware Design Guidelines](https://www.espressif.com/hardware-design-guidelines) Section General Principles of PCB Layout for Modules.
