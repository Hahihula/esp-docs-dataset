```markdown
Register 11.20. PMU_HP_MODEM_BIAS_REG (0x004C)

PMU_HP_MODEM_XPD_BIAS Configures the power supply of BIAS in HP_MODEM state. (R/W)
PMU_HP_MODEM_DBG_ATTEN Configures the degree of attenuation of the analog band gap in
HP_MODEM state. (R/W)
PMU_HP_MODEM_PD_CUR Configures the power-down current in HP_MODEM state. (R/W)
PMU_HP_MODEM_BIAS_SLEEP Configures the sleep or wakeup status of the BIAS circuit in
HP_MODEM state. (R/W)
```
