Register 7.63. LP_CLKRST_LP_CLK_CONF_REG (0x0000)

LP_CLKRST_SLOW_CLK_SEL   Configures the source of LP_SLOW_CLK.
    0: RC_SLOW_CLK
    1: XTAL32K_CLK
    2: reserved
    3: OSC_SLOW_CLK
    (R/W)

LP_CLKRST_FAST_CLK_SEL   Configures the source of LP_FAST_CLK.
    0: RC_FAST_CLK
    1: XTAL_D2_CLK
    2: XTAL_CLK
    (R/W)
