Register 11.25. PMU_HP_MODEM_HP_REGULATOR1_REG (0x0060)

PMU_HP_MODEM_HP_REGULATOR_DRV_B   Configures the drive strength of the main HP regulator in HP_MODEM state. (R/W)

Register 11.26. PMU_HP_MODEM_XTAL_REG (0x0064)

PMU_HP_MODEM_XPD_XTAL   Configures whether to enable XTAL_CLK in HP_MODEM state.
O: Disable
1: Enable
(R/W)
