## 18.7.2 Other Registers

Register 18.16. BUS_MONITOR_CORE_O_MONTR_ENA_REG (0x0000)

BUS_MONITOR_CORE_O_AREA_DRAMO_O_RD_ENA Configures whether to monitor read operations in region O by the Data bus.
- O: Not monitor
- 1: Monitor
(R/W)

BUS_MONITOR_CORE_O_AREA_DRAMO_O_WR_ENA Configures whether to monitor write operations in region O by the Data bus.
- O: Not monitor
- 1: Monitor
(R/W)

BUS_MONITOR_CORE_O_AREA_DRAMO_1_RD_ENA Configures whether to monitor read operations in region 1 by the Data bus.
- O: Not Monitor
- 1: Monitor
(R/W)

BUS_MONITOR_CORE_O_AREA_DRAMO_1_WR_ENA Configures whether to monitor write operations in region 1 by the Data bus.
- O: Not Monitor
- 1: Monitor
(R/W)

BUS_MONITOR_CORE_O_AREA_PIF_O_RD_ENA Configures whether to monitor read operations in region O by the Peripheral bus.
- O: Not Monitor
- 1: Monitor
(R/W)
