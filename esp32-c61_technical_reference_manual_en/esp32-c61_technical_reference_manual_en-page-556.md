Register 11.48. PMU_LP_SLEEP_LP_CK_POWER_REG (0x00C4)

PMU_LP_SLEEP_XPD_XTAL32K   Configures whether to power up XTAL32K_CLK in LP_SLEEP state.
    0: Power down
    1: Power up
    (R/W)

PMU_LP_SLEEP_XPD_RC32K     Configures whether to power up RC32K_CLK in LP_SLEEP state.
    0: Power down
    1: Power up
    (R/W)

PMU_LP_SLEEP_XPD_FOSC_CLK  Configures whether to power up RC_FAST_CLK in LP_SLEEP state.
    0: Power down
    1: Power up
    (R/W)

PMU_LP_SLEEP_PD_OSC_CLK    Configures whether to power up SOSC_CLK in LP_SLEEP state.
    0: Power down
    1: Power up
    (R/W)
