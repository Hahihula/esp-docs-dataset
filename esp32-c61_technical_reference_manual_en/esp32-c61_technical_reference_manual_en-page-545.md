Register 11.33. PMU_HP_SLEEP_BIAS_REG (0x0080)

PMU_HP_SLEEP_XPD_BIAS   Configures the power supply of BIAS in HP_SLEEP state. (R/W)
PMU_HP_SLEEP_DBG_ATTEN Configures the degree of attenuation of the analog band gap in
HP_SLEEP state. (R/W)
PMU_HP_SLEEP_PD_CUR    Configures the power-down current in HP_SLEEP state. (R/W)
PMU_HP_SLEEP_BIAS_SLEEP Configures the sleep or wakeup status of the BIAS circuit in
HP_SLEEP state. (R/W)
