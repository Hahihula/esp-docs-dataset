

```markdown
## Register 7.46. PCR_CACHE_CONF_REG (0x00D8)

PCR_CACHE_CLK_EN Configures whether or not to enable Cache clock.
- O: Not enable
- 1: Enable
(R/W)

PCR_CACHE_RST_EN Configures whether or not to reset Cache.
- O: Not reset
- 1: Reset
(R/W)
```

```markdown
## Register 7.47. PCR_CACHE_PD_CTRL_REG (0x00DC)

PCR_CACHE_MEM_FORCE_PU Configures whether or not to force power up Cache internal memory.
- O: Not force power up
- 1: Force power up
(R/W)

PCR_CACHE_MEM_FORCE_PD Configures whether or not to force power down Cache internal memory.
- O: Not force power down
- 1: Force power down
(R/W)
```
