Register 11.38. PMU_HP_SLEEP_HP_REGULATOR1_REG (0x0094)

PMU_HP_SLEEP_HP_REGULATOR_DRV_B   Configures the drive strength of the main HP regulator in HP_SLEEP state. (R/W)

Register 11.39. PMU_HP_SLEEP_XTAL_REG (0x0098)

PMU_HP_SLEEP_XPD_XTAL   Configures whether to enable XTAL_CLK in HP_SLEEP state.
    O: Disable
    1: Enable
(R/W)
