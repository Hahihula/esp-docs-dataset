- Digital power controller: powers up or down the digital power domains.
- Analog power controller: enables the analog modules, such as the regulators, analog clocks, etc.
- Clock controller: manages the clock gating of peripherals and selects analog clock sources for digital clocks.
- Data backup controller: controls the data backup and restore process when the chip switches between PMU states.
- System controller: controls some system-level modules, such as suspending watchdog functionality or handling backups in sleep mode (when the CPU in the HP system is unavailable).

The PMU workflow involves the sleep/wake-up controller sending sleep or wake-up requests to the PMU main state machine, which then generates power gating, clock gating, and reset signals. The power controllers and clock controller will then power up or down different power domains and clocks based on the signals generated by the PMU main state machine, allowing the chip to enter or exit different low-power modes.
