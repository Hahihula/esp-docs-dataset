Title: Datasheet Versioning

Table:
- Column Headers: "Datasheet Version", "Status", "Watermark", "Definition"
- Row 1: 
  - Datasheet Version: v0.1 ~ v0.5 (excluding v0.5)
  - Status: Draft
  - Watermark: Confidential
  - Definition: This datasheet is under development for products in the design stage. Specifications may change without prior notice.
- Row 2:
  - Datasheet Version: v0.5 ~ v1.0 (excluding v1.0) release
  - Status: Preliminary
  - Watermark: Preliminary
  - Definition: This datasheet is actively updated for products in the verification stage. Specifications may change before mass production, and the changes will be documented in the datasheet’s Revision History.
- Row 3:
  - Datasheet Version: v1.0 and higher Official release —
  - Status: 
  - Watermark: 
  - Definition: This datasheet is publicly released for products in mass production. Specifications are finalized, and major changes will be communicated via Product Change Notifications (PCN).
- Row 4:
  - Datasheet Version: Any version Recommended for New Design —
  - Status: Not NRND^1
  - Watermark: 
  - Definition: This datasheet is updated less frequently for products not recommended for new designs.
- Row 5:
  - Datasheet Version: Any version End of Life (EOL)^2
  - Status: 
  - Watermark: 
  - Definition: This datasheet is no longer maintained for products that have reached end of life.

Footnotes:
1. "Watermark will be added to the datasheet title page only when all the product variants covered by this datasheet are not recommended for new designs."
2. "Watermark will be added to the datasheet title page only when all the product variants covered by this datasheet have reached end of life."
