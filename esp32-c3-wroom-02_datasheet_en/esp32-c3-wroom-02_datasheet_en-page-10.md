Subsection Title: 3.1 Pin Layout

Body Text:
The pin diagram below shows the approximate location of pins on the module. For the actual diagram drawn to scale, please refer to Figure 10.1 Module Dimensions.

Image Description (Pin Diagram):
- The image is a schematic representation showing various labeled components and connections.
- There's an area marked as "Keepout Zone" with dotted lines around it indicating where certain pins are located relative to this zone on the module layout diagram, specifically for ESP32-C3-WROOM-02U.

Note A:
The text in Note A is highlighted within a blue box. It states: 
"The zone marked with dotted lines is the antenna keepout zone. The pin diagram is applicable to ESP32-C3-WROOM-02 and ESP32-C3-WROOM-02U, but the latter has no antenna keepout zone.
To learn more about the keepout zone for module’s antenna on the base board, please refer to
ESP32-C3 Hardware Design Guidelines > Section General Principles of PCB Layout for Modules."
