**1.2 Series Comparison**

ESP32-C3-WROOM-02 and ESP32-C3-WROOM-02U are two general-purpose Wi-Fi and Bluetooth LE modules with a rich set of peripherals, making them ideal for smart homes, industrial automation, health care, consumer electronics.

Both models feature an external SPI flash. The 02 model comes with on-board PCB antenna while the U version has connector support for additional antennas.
