Chapter 8 Reset and Clock

The clock source of LP_DYN_SLOW_CLK is LP_SLOW_CLK.

The clock source of LP_DYN_FAST_CLK depends on the chip’s power mode (see Chapter 12 Low-Power Management).

* Select LP_FAST_CLK as its clock source in Active and Modem-sleep mode
* Select LP_SLOW_CLK as its clock source in Light-sleep and Deep-sleep mode
