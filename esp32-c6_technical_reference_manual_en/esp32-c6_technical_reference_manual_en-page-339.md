Register 8.31. PCR_SARADC_CONF_REG (0x0080)

PCR_SARADC_RST_EN Configures whether or not to reset function register of SAR ADC module.
O: Not reset
1: Reset
(R/W)

PCR_SARADC_APB_CLK_EN Configures whether or not to enable SAR ADC APB clock.
O: Not enable
1: Enable
(R/W)

PCR_SARADC_APB_RST_EN Configures whether or not to reset APB register of SAR ADC module.
O: Not reset
1: Reset
(R/W)
