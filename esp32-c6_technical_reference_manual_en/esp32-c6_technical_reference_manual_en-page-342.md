Register 8.35. PCR_INTMTX_CONF_REG (0x0090)

PCR_INTMTX_CLK_EN Configures whether or not to enable Interrupt Matrix clock.
O: Not enable
1: Enable
(R/W)

PCR_INTMTX_RST_EN Configures whether or not to reset Interrupt Matrix module.
O: Not reset
1: Reset
(R/W)

Register 8.36. PCR_PCNT_CONF_REG (0x0094)

PCR_PCNT_CLK_EN Configures whether or not to enable PCNT clock.
O: Not enable
1: Enable
(R/W)

PCR_PCNT_RST_EN Configures whether or not to reset PCNT module.
O: Not reset
1: Reset
(R/W)
