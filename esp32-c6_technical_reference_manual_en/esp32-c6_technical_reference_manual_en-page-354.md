```markdown
## Register 8.57. PCR_MEM_MONITOR_CONF_REG (0x00F0)

PCR_MEM_MONITOR_CLK_EN Configures whether or not to enable MEM_MONITOR clock.
- O: Not enable
- 1: Enable
(R/W)

PCR_MEM_MONITOR_RST_EN Configures whether or not to reset MEM_MONITOR module.
- O: Not reset
- 1: Reset
(R/W)
```
