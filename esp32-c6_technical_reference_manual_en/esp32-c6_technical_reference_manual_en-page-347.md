```markdown
## Register 8.43. PCR_SDIO_SLAVE_CONF_REG (0x00B0)

PCR_SDIO_SLAVE_CLK_EN Configures whether or not to enable SDIO slave clock.
- O: Not enable
- 1: Enable
(R/W)

PCR_SDIO_SLAVE_RST_EN Configures whether or not to reset SDIO slave module.
- O: Not reset
- 1: Reset
(R/W)
```
