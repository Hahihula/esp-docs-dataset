```markdown
Register 39.8. APB_SARADC_SAR1DATA_STATUS_REG (0x002C)

APB_SARADC_DATA Represents SAR ADC conversion data. (RO)
```

```markdown
Register 39.9. APB_SARADC_THRESO_CTRL_REG (0x0034)

APB_SARADC_THRESO_CHANNEL Configures the channel for SAR ADC monitor O. (R/W)
APB_SARADC_THRESO_HIGH Configures the high threshold for SAR ADC monitor O. (R/W)
APB_SARADC_THRESO_LOW Configures the low threshold for SAR ADC monitor O. (R/W)
```

```markdown
Register 39.10. APB_SARADC_THRESH1_CTRL_REG (0x0038)

APB_SARADC_THRESH1_CHANNEL Configures the channel for SAR ADC monitor 1. (R/W)
APB_SARADC_THRESH1_HIGH Configures the high threshold for SAR ADC monitor 1. (R/W)
APB_SARADC_THRESH1_LOW Configures the low threshold for SAR ADC monitor 1. (R/W)
```
