

```markdown
Register 8.51. PCR_ECC_CONF_REG (0x00D8)

PCR_ECC_CLK_EN Configures whether or not to enable ECC clock.
O: Not enable
1: Enable
(R/W)

PCR_ECC_RST_EN Configures whether or not to reset ECC module.
O: Not reset
1: Reset
(R/W)
```

```markdown
Register 8.52. PCR_ECC_PD_CTRL_REG (0x00DC)

PCR_ECC_MEM_PD Configures whether or not to power down ECC internal memory.
O: Not power down
1: Power down
(R/W)

PCR_ECC_MEM_FORCE_PU Configures whether or not to force power up ECC internal memory.
O: Not force power up
1: Force power up
(R/W)

PCR_ECC_MEM_FORCE_PD Configures whether or not to force power down ECC internal memory.
O: Not force power down
1: Force power down
(R/W)
```
