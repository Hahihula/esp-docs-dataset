

```markdown
Register 8.9. PCR_UHCI_CONF_REG (0x0028)

PCR_UHCI_CLK_EN Configures whether or not to enable UHCI clock.
O: Not enable
1: Enable
(R/W)

PCR_UHCI_RST_EN Configures whether or not to reset UHCI module.
O: Not reset
1: Reset
(R/W)
```

Register 8.10. PCR_RMT_CONF_REG (0x002C)

PCR_RMT_CLK_EN Configures whether or not to enable RMT APB clock.
O: Enable
1: Not enable
(R/W)

PCR_RMT_RST_EN Configures whether or not to reset RMT module.
