```markdown
## Register 8.47. PCR_AES_CONF_REG (0x00C8)

PCR_AES_CLK_EN Configures whether or not to enable AES clock.
- O: Not enable
- 1: Enable
(R/W)

PCR_AES_RST_EN Configures whether or not to reset AES module.
- O: Not reset
- 1: Reset
(R/W)
```
