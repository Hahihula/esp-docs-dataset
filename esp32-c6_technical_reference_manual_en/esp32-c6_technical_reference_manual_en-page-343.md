

```markdown
Register 8.37. PCR_ETM_CONF_REG (0x0098)

PCR_ETM_CLK_EN Configures whether or not to enable ETM clock.
O: Not enable
1: Enable
(R/W)

PCR_ETM_RST_EN Configures whether or not to reset ETM module.
O: Not reset
1: Reset
(R/W)
```
