Register 8.84. LP_CLKRST_LPPERI_REG (0x0028)

LP_CLKRST_LP_UART_CLK_SEL   Configures the source clk of LP UART.
    O: LP_FAST_CLK
    1: XTAL_D2_CLK
    (R/W)

LP_CLKRST_LP_I2C_CLK_SEL   Configures the source clk of LP I2C.
    O: LP_FAST_CLK
    1: XTAL_D2_CLK
    (R/W)
