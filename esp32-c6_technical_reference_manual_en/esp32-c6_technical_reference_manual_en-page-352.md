```markdown
## Register 8.53. PCR_DS_CONF_REG (0x00E0)

PCR_DS_CLK_EN Configures whether or not to enable DS clock.
- O: Not enable
- 1: Enable
(R/W)

PCR_DS_RST_EN Configures whether or not to reset DS module.
- O: Not reset
- 1: Reset
(R/W)
```
