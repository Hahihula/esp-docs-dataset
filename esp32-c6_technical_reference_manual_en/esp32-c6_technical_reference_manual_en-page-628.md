ASSIST_DEBUG_CORE_O_AREA_PIF_1_MIN    Configures the lower bound address of Peripheral bus region 1. (R/W)

ASSIST_DEBUG_CORE_O_AREA_PIF_1_MAX    Configures the upper bound address of Peripheral bus region 1. (R/W)


Register 18.22. ASSIST_DEBUG_CORE_O_AREA_PC_REG (0x0030)

ASSIST_DEBUG_CORE_O_AREA_PC    Represents the PC value when an interrupt is triggered during region monitoring. (RO)
