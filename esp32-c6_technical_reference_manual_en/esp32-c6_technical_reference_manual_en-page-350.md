

```markdown
Register 8.49. PCR_RSA_CONF_REG (0x00D0)

PCR_RSA_CLK_EN Configures whether or not to enable RSA clock.
O: Not enable
1: Enable
(R/W)

PCR_RSA_RST_EN Configures whether or not to reset RSA module.
O: Not reset
1: Reset
(R/W)
```

```markdown
Register 8.50. PCR_RSA_PD_CTRL_REG (0x00D4)

PCR_RSA_MEM_PD Configures whether or not to power down RSA internal memory.
O: Not power down
1: Power down
(R/W)

PCR_RSA_MEM_FORCE_PU Configures whether or not to force power up RSA internal memory.
O: Not force power up
1: Force power up
(R/W)

PCR_RSA_MEM_FORCE_PD Configures whether or not to force power down RSA internal memory.
O: Not force power down
1: Force power down
(R/W)
```
