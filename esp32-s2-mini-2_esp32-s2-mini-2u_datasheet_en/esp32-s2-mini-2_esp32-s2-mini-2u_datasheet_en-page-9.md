**Figure Caption (Top):**
- Figure 2-1. ESP32-S2-MINI-2 Block Diagram

**Block Diagram Components for Top Diagram:**
- **ESP32-S2FH4, ESP32-S2FN4R2**: These are the components inside a dashed box.
- **40 MHz Crystal**: Connected to "ESP32-S2FH4" and labeled as such within the diagram.
- **RF Matching**: Positioned between two antennas in separate corners of the block. 
- **Antenna**: Two antenna symbols, one on each side outside the main component area.

**Connections:**
- 3V3 (top left)
- EN
- FLASH (QSPI), PSRAM(opt.) (QSPI)
