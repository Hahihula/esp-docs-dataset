In this datasheet unless otherwise stated, ESP32-S2-MINI-2 refers to all variants of ESP32-S2-MINI-2, whereas ESP32-S2-MINI-2U refers to all variants of ESP32-S2-MINI-2U. 

At the core of the modules is ESP32-S2 series. ESP32-S2 series of chips has an Xtensa® 32-bit LX7 CPU that operates at up to 240 MHz. It has a low-power co-processor that can be used instead of the CPU to save power while performing tasks that do not require much computing power, such as monitoring of peripherals.
