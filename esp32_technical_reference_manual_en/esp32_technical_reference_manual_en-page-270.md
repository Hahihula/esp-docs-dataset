**Subtitle and Register Information:**
Register 12.201. DPORT_SPI_DMA_CHAN_SEL_REG (0x5A8)

**Binary Representation Diagram with Labels:**
- The diagram shows a binary representation of the register, labeled from bit positions to bits.
- Bits are numbered starting at '3' on one side and ending just before '0' in reverse order.

**Descriptions for Each Bit Field:**
1. **DPOR_SPI_SPI3_DMA_CHAN_SEL**: Selects the DMA channel for SPI3 (R/W)
2. **DPOR_SPI_SPI2_DMA_CHAN_SEL**: Selects the DMA channel for SPI2 (R/W)
3. **DPOR_SPI_SPI1_DMA_CHAN_SEL**: Selects the DMA channel for SPI1 (R/W)
