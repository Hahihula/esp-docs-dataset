**Table Title:**
Register 9.28. RTC_CNTL_DIG_PWC_REG (0x0084)

**Table Content Description:**
The table lists various control registers related to power management in the RTC_CNTL DIG PWC register, with each column representing a specific bit or group of bits within this register.

**Text Entries and Descriptions from Table:**

1. **RTC_CNTL_DG_WRAPE_PD_EN**: Enable power down digital core in sleep mode.
2. **RTC_CNTL_WIFI_PD_EN**: Enable power down Wi-Fi in sleep (R/W).
3. **RTC_CNTL_INTER_RAM4_PD_EN**: Enable power down internal SRAM 4 in sleep mode.
4. **RTC_CNTL_INTER_RAM3_PD_EN**: Enable power down internal SRAM 3 in sleep mode.
5. **RTC_CNTL_INTER_RAM2_PD_EN**: Enable power down internal SRAM 2 in sleep mode (R/W).
6. **RTC_CNTL_INTER_RAM1_PD_EN**: Enable power down internal SRAM 1 in sleep mode.

7. **RTC_CNTL_INTER_RAMO_PD_EN**: Enable power down internal SRAM 0 in sleep mode.
8. **RTC_CNTL_ROMO_PD_EN**: Enable power down ROM in sleep mode (R/W).

9. **RTC_CNTL_DG_WRAPEFU**: Digital core force power up (R/W).
10. **RTC_CNTL_DG_WRAPE_PD**: Digital core force power down.

11. **RTC_CNTL_WIFI_FORCED_PU**: Wi-Fi force power up.
12. **RTC_CNTL_WIFI_FORCED_PD**: Wi-Fi force power down.

13. **RTC_CNTL_INTER_RAM4_FORCEDFU**: Internal SRAM 4 force power up (R/W).
14. **RTC_CNTL_INTER_RAM4_FORCED_PD**: Internal SRAM 4 force power down (R/W).

15. **RTC_CNTL_INTER_RAM3_FORCEDFU**: Internal SRAM 3 force power up.
16. **RTC_CNTL_INTER_RAM3_FORCED_PD**: Internal SRAM 3 force power down.

17. **RTC_CNTL_INTER_RAM2_FORCEDFU**: Internal SRAM 2 force power up (R/W).
18. **RTC_CNTL_INTER_RAM2_FORCED_PD**: Internal SRAM 2 force power down.
19. **RTC_CNTL_INTER_RAM1_FORCEDFU**: Internal SRAM 1 force power up.

20. **RTC_CNTL_INTER_RAM1_FORCED_PD**: Internal SRAM 1 force power down (R/W).

21. **RTC_CNTL_INTER_RAMO_FORCEDFU**: Internal SRAM 0 force power up.
22. **RTC_CNTL_INTER_RAMO_FORCED_PD**: Internal SRAM 0 force power down.
