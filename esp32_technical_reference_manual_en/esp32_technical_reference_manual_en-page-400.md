**Title:**
Chapter 21 I2C Controller (I2C)

**Body Text:**

- **I2C_TXFIFO_EMPTY_INT:** I2C TX FIFO interrupt. Triggered when the pointers of TX FIFO are less than `I2C_TXFIFO EMPTY THRD[4:0]`.
  
- **I2C_RXFIFO_FULL_INT:** I2C RX FIFO interrupt. Triggered when the pointers of RX FIFO are greater than `I2C_RXFIFO FULL THRD[4:0]`.
