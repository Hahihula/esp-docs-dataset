**Section Title: Register 9.4. RTC_CNTL_TIME_UPDATE_REG (0x00C)**

- **Field Name:** RTC_CNTL_TIME_UPDATE
- **Description:** Set 1: to update register with RTC timer.
- **Access Mode:** WO
- **Field Name:** RTC_CNTL_TIME_VALID
- **Description:** Indicates that the register is updated. 
- **Access Mode:** RO

---

**Section Title: Register 9.5. RTC_CNTL_TIME0_REG (0x010)**

- **Field Name:** RTC_CNTL_TIMEO_REG
- **Description:** RTC timer low 32 bits.
- **Access Mode:** RO

---

**Section Title: Register 9.6. RTC_CNTL_TIME1_REG (0x0014)**

- **Field Name:** RTC_CNTL_TIME_HI
- **Description:** RTC timer high 16 bits.
