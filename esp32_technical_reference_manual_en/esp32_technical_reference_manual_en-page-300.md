**Title: Chapter 16 SHA Accelerator (SHA)**

---

**Section Title:** Register 16.11. SHA_SHA384_CONTINUE_REG (0xA4)

- **Description:** 
  - **Field Name:** SHA_SHA384_CONTINUE
  - **Type:** Writeable register to continue the SHA-384 operation with subsequent blocks.
  - **Access Mode:** WO

**Register Description:**
- **Name:** Register 16.12. SHA_SHA384_LOAD_REG (0xA8)
- **Field Name:** (reserved)

---

**Section Title:** Register 16.13. SHA_SHA384_BUY_REG (0xAC)

- **Description:**
  - **Field Name:** SHA_SHA384_BUY
  - **Type:** Read-only register to show the status of the SHA accelerator.
  - **Access Mode:** RO

**Register Description:**
- **Name:** Register 16.12. SHA_SHA384_LOAD_REG (0xA8)
- **Field Name:** (reserved)
