**Title:**
Chapter 10 Low-power Management (RTC_CNTL)

**Subtitles and Body Text with Descriptions of Registers:**

- **Register 10.33. RTC_CNTL_RTC_WDTCONFIG1_REG (0x009C)**
  - Description:
    - RTC_CNTL_WDT_STGO_HOLD
      - Configures the hold time of RTC watchdog at level 1.
      - Access Type: Read/Write

- **Register 10.34. RTC_CNTL_RTC_WDTCONFIG2_REG (0x00A0)**
  - Description:
    - RTC_CNTL_WDT_STG1_HOLD
      - Configures the hold time of RTC watchdog at level 2.
      - Access Type: Read/Write

- **Register 10.35. RTC_CNTL_RTC_WDTCONFIG3_REG (0x00A4)**
  - Description:
    - RTC_CNTL_WDT_STG2_HOLD
      - Configures the hold time of RTC watchdog at level 3.
      - Access Type: Read/Write
