### Register 5.29. EFUSE_RD_SYS_PART1_DATA5_REG (0x0070)

- **EFUSE_sys_data_part1_1**: Represents the second 32 bits of the second part of system data.
  - **(RO)**

---

### Register 5.30. EFUSE_RD_SYS_PART1_DATA6_REG (0x0074)

- **EFUSE_sys_data_part1_2**: Represents the third 32 bits of the second part of system data.
  - **(RO)**

---

### Register 5.31. EFUSE_RD_SYS_PART1_DATA7_REG (0x0078)

- **EFUSE_sys_data_part1_3**: Represents the fourth 32 bits of the second part of system data.
  - **(RO)**
