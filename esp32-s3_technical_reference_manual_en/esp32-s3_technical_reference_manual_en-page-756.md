**Title:**
Register 15.46, PMS_CORE_X_DRAMO_PMS CONSTRAINT_1_REG (0x0100)

**Body Text with Descriptions of Registers and Their Functions:**

- **PMS_CORE_X_DRAMO_PMS CONSTRAINT_SRAM_WORLD_0_PMS_0**: Configures the permission of CPU’s DBUS to instruction region of SRAM from the Secure World. It's advised to configure this field to 0 (R/W).

- **PMS_CORE_X_DRAMO_PMS CONSTRAINT_SRAM_WORLD_0_PMS_1**: Configures the permission of CPU’s DBUS to data region9 of SRAM from the Secure World. (R/W)

- **PMS_CORE_X_DRAMO_PMS CONSTRAINT_SRAM_WORLD_0_PMS_2**: Configures the permission of CPU’s DBUS to data region1 of SRAM from the Secure World. (R/W)
