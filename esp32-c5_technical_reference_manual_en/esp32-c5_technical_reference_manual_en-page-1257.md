Register 34.37. LP_I2C_SDA_HOLD_REG (0x0030)

LP_I2C_SDA_HOLD_TIME Configures the time to hold the data after the falling edge of SCL.
Measurement unit: I2C_SCLK clock cycles
(R/W)
