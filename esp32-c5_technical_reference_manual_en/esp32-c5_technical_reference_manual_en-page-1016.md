(RO)

KEYMNG_PROC_DONE_INT_ST The masked interrupt status of KEYMNG_PROC_DONE_INT interrupt.
(RO)

KEYMNG_POST_DONE_INT_ST The masked interrupt status of KEYMNG_POST_DONE_INT interrupt.
(RO)
