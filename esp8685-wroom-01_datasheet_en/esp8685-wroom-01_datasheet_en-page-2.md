**Section 1. Features**

* **CPU and On-Chip Memory**
  - ESP8685H4 embedded, 32-bit RISC-V single-core processor, up to 160 MHz
  - 384 KB ROM
  - 400 KB SRAM (16 KB for cache)
  - 8 KB SRAM in RTC
  - 4 MB flash in chip package

* **Wi-Fi**
  - IEEE 802.11b/g/n-compliant
  - Center frequency range of operating channel: 2412 ~ 2484 MHz
  - Supports 20 MHz, 40 MHz bandwidth in the 2.4 GHz band

* **Peripheral Features**
  - Up to 15 GPIOs (General Purpose Input/Output pins)
    - Strapping GPIOs for various functions like CAN Specification and temperature sensor.
  - SPI, UART, I2C, I2S interfaces
  - Remote control peripheral with LED PWM controller.

**Section: Integrated Components on Module**
- On-board PCB antenna

**Section: Antenna Options**

* **Peripheral Features (continued)**
  - Wi-Fi Multimedia (WMM)
  - TX/RX A-MPDU, RX/A MSDU for data aggregation
  - Immediate Block ACK support.
  - Fragmentation and defragmentation capabilities.
