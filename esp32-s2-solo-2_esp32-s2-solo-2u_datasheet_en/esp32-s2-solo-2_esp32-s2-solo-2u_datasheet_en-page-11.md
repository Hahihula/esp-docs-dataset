Subsection Title: 3.1 Pin Layout

Body Text:
The pin diagram below shows the approximate location of pins on the module. For the actual diagram drawn to scale, please refer to Figure 9.1 Module Dimensions.

Image Description (Figure Caption):
- The image is a schematic representation labeled "Figure 3-1. Pin Layout (Top View)" showing various labels for different components and pin numbers.
- Labels include: GND, IO0 through IO45 with corresponding numerical positions from top to bottom on the left side of each row.

Note:
The zone marked with dotted lines is the antenna keepout zone. The pin diagram is applicable to ESP32-S2-SOLO-2 and ESP32-S2-SOLO-2U, but the latter has no antenna keepout zone.
To learn more about the keepout zone for module’s antenna on the base board, please refer to
[ESP32-S2 Hardware Design Guidelines](#) > Section General Principles of PCB Layout for Modules.
