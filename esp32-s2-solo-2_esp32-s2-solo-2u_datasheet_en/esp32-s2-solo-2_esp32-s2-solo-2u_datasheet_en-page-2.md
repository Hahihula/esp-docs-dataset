**1.1 Features**

* **CPU and On-Chip Memory**
  - ESP32-S2 or ESP32-S2R2 embedded, Xtensa® single-core 32-bit LX7 microprocessor, up to 240 MHz
  - 128 KB ROM
  - 320 KB SRAM
  - 16 KB SRAM in RTC
  - 2 MB PSRAM (ESP32-S2R2 only)

* **Wi-Fi**
  - 802.11b/g/n
    - Bit rate: 802.11n up to 150 Mbps
    - A-MPDU and A-MSDU aggregation
    - 0.4 µs guard interval support

  - Center frequency range of operating channel:
    - 2412 ~ 2484 MHz

* **Peripherals**
  - Up to 36 GPIOs
  - SPI, LCD, UART, I2C, I2S, Camera interface, IR, pulse counter, LED PWM, TWAI® (compatible with ISO 11898-1), i.e. CAN Specification 2.0,
    - full-speed USB OTG, ADC, DAC, touch sensor, temperature sensor, general-purpose timers, watchdog timers

**Integrated Components on Module**

- 40 MHz crystal oscillator
- Quad SPI flash up to 16 MB

---

**Antenna Options**

- **ESP32-S2-SOLO-2:** On-board PCB antenna connector
- **ESP32-S2-SOLO-2U:** External antenna via a connector

---

**Operating Conditions**

- Operating voltage/Power supply: 3.0 ~ 3.6 V
- Operating ambient temperature:
  - 85 °C version: –40 ~ 85 °C
  - 105 °C version: –40 ~ 105 °C
