**Additional Text (Subsection):**
11.4 Ultrasonic Vibration

- Avoid exposing Espressif modules to vibration from ultrasonic equipment such as ultrasonic welders or ultrasonic cleaners.
- This vibration may induce resonance in the in-module crystal and lead to its malfunction or even failure, causing potential stoppage of module operation or deterioration in performance.
