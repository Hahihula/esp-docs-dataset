Register 20.36. HP_SYSTEM_HP_L1CACHE_BUS0_ID_REG (0x00C8)

HP_SYSTEM_L1_CACHE_BUS0_ID Configures the L1 cache bus0 UID. (R/W)


Register 20.37. HP_SYSTEM_HP_L1CACHE_BUS1_ID_REG (0x00CC)

HP_SYSTEM_L1_CACHE_BUS1_ID Configures the L1 cache bus1 UID. (R/W)


Register 20.38. HP_SYSTEM_GPIO_DED_HOLD_CTRL_REG (0x00F0)

HP_SYSTEM_GPIO_DED_HOLD Configures whether or not to hold the pin status.
Lower 6 bits: flash pins
Higher 20 bits: 16 line PSRAM pins
(R/W)
