Register 9.36. GPIO_ZERO_DETO_FILTER_CNT_REG (0x0710)

GPIO_ZERO_DETO_FILTER_CNT   Configures to change GPIO analog comparator0 zero detect filter’s threshold.
Interrupts in GPIO_ZERO_DETO_FILTER_CNT iomux_core_clk cycles after one interrupt will be filtered, and not be seen by CPU. (R/W)
