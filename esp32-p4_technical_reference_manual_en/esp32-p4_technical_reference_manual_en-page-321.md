```markdown
Register 4.96. AXI_DMA_OUT_CRC_CLEAR_CHn_REG (n: 0-2) (0x0188+0x68*n)

AXI_DMA_OUT_CRC_CLEAR_CHn_REG Configures whether to clear the CRC result for TX channel n.

O: Not clear
I: Clear
(R/W)
```

```markdown
Register 4.97. AXI_DMA_OUT_CRC_FINAL_RESULT_CHn_REG (n: 0-2) (0x018C+0x68*n)

AXI_DMA_OUT_CRC_FINAL_RESULT_CHn Represents the CRC result for TX channel n. (RO)
```
