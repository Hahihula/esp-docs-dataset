Register 20.91. LP_SYSTEM_LP_CORE_BOOT_ADDR_REG (0x0018)

LP_SYSTEM_LP_CPU_BOOT_ADDR Configures the LP CPU boot address. (R/W)


Register 20.92. LP_SYSTEM_LP_SPM_PWR_CTRL_REG (0x0024)

LP_SYSTEM_LP_SPM_ROM_CLK_FORCE_ON Configures whether or not to force on LP SPM_ROM clock when LP CPU clock is on.
0: Not force on
1: Force on
(R/W)

LP_SYSTEM_LP_SPM_RAM_CLK_FORCE_ON Configures whether or not to force on LP SPM_RAM clock.
0: Not force on
1: Force on
(R/W)
