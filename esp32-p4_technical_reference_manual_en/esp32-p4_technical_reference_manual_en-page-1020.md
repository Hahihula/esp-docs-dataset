Register 14.21. PMU_HP_SLEEP_LP_REGULATOR0_REG (0x009C)

PMU_HP_SLEEP_LP_REGULATOR_XPD Configures whether to enable LP sys regulator in HP_ACTIVE/HP_SLEEP state.
O: Disable
1: Enable
(R/W)

PMU_HP_SLEEP_LP_REGULATOR_DBIAS Regulates the voltage of the LP sys regulator in HP_ACTIVE/HP_SLEEP state. The higher the value, the higher the voltage. (R/W)
