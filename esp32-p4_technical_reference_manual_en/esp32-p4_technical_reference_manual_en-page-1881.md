

```markdown
Register 39.21. H264_A_ROI_CONFIG_REG (0x0050)

H264_A_ROI_EN Configures whether to enable ROI in video sequence A.
O: Disable ROI
1: Enable ROI
(R/W)

H264_A_ROI_MODE Configures the mode of ROI in video sequence A.
O: Fixed QP
1: Delta QP
(R/W)
```

```markdown
Register 39.22. H264_B_SYS_MB_RES_REG (0x0054)

H264_B_SYS_TOTAL_MB_Y Configures video sequence B vertical MB resolution. (R/W)

H264_B_SYS_TOTAL_MB_X Configures video sequence B horizontal MB resolution. (R/W)
```
