DMA2D_OUT_COLOR_PARAM_H1_CHn   Configures the coefficient C and D for the most significant byte of the input of color space conversion’s second stage for TX channel n. (R/W)

DMA2D_OUT_COLOR_PARAM_M1_CHn   Configures the coefficient C and D for the middle byte of the input of color space conversion’s second stage for TX channel n. (R/W)
