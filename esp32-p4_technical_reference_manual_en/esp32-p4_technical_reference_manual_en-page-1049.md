Register 14.54. PMU_LP_CPU_PWR3_REG (0x0190)

PMU_LP_CPU_WAKEUP_CAUSE Indicates the wake-up cause of LP CPU. (RO)


Register 14.55. PMU_LP_CPU_PWR4_REG (0x0194)

PMU_LP_CPU_REJECT_EN Configures whether to enable sleep rejection function for the LP CPU.
O: Disable
1: Enable
(R/W)


Register 14.56. PMU_LP_CPU_PWR5_REG (0x0198)

PMU_LP_CPU_REJECT_CAUSE Indicates the sleep rejection cause of LP CPU. (RO)
