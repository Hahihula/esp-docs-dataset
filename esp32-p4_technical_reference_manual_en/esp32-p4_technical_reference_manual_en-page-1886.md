H264_B_ROI_REGION1_X Configures the horizontal start MBs of ROI 1 in Video sequence B. (R/W)

H264_B_ROI_REGION1_Y Configures the vertical start MBs of ROI 1 in Video sequence B. (R/W)

H264_B_ROI_REGION1_X_LEN Configures the number of MBs in horizontal direction of the ROI 1 in Video sequence B. (R/W)

H264_B_ROI_REGION1_Y_LEN Configures the number of MBs in vertical direction of the ROI 1 in Video sequence B. (R/W)

H264_B_ROI_REGION1_EN Configures whether to enable Video sequence B ROI 1.
0: Disable ROI
1: Enable ROI
(R/W)
