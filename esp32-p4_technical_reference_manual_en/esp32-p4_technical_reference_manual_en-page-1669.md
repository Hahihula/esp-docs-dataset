Chapter 36 Image Signal Processor (ISP)

Register 36.2. ISP_CLK_EN_REG (0x0004)

Continued from the previous page...

ISP_CLK_MIPI_IDI_FORCE_ON Configures whether to force enable the MIPI Image Interface 32 input clock.
O: Not force enable
1: Force enable
(R/W)

ISP_MEM_CLK_FORCE_ON Configures whether to force enable all the ISP memory clocks.
O: Not force enable
1: Force enable
(R/W)

ISP_CLK_CROP_FORCE_ON Configures whether to force enable the CROP clock.
O: Not force enable
1: Force enable
(R/W)

ISP_CLK_WBG_FORCE_ON Configures whether to force enable the WBG clock.
O: Not force enable
1: Force enable
(R/W)
