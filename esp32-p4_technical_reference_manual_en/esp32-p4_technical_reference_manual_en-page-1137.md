Chapter 18 RTC Timer

Register 18.16. RTC_TIMER_LP_INT_ENA_REG (0x0040)

RTC_TIMER_MAIN_TIMER_OVERFLOW_LP_INT_ENA   Configure to enable the interrupt for the 48-bit counter overflow. (R/W)

RTC_TIMER_MAIN_TIMER_LP_INT_ENA   Configure to enable the interrupt for the RTC Timer reaching the target time 1. (R/W)

Register 18.17. RTC_TIMER_LP_INT_CLR_REG (0x0044)

RTC_TIMER_MAIN_TIMER_OVERFLOW_LP_INT_CLR   Configure to clear the interrupt for 48-bit counter overflow. (WO)

RTC_TIMER_MAIN_TIMER_LP_INT_CLR   Configure to clear the interrupt for the RTC Timer reaching the target time 1. (WO)
