Register 20.63. HP_SYSTEM_L2_MEM_REFRESH_REG (0x0164)

Continued from the previous page...

HP_SYSTEM_L2_MEM_UNIT5_REFERSH_EN Configures whether or not to refresh the ECC bit of L2MEM unit5.
O: Disable
1: Enable
(R/W)

HP_SYSTEM_L2_MEM_REFERSH_CNT_RESET Configures whether or not to reset L2MEM refresh count.
O: No effect
1: Reset
(R/W)

HP_SYSTEM_L2_MEM_UNIT0_REFRESH_DONE Represents the refresh status of L2MEM unit0 ECC bit. (RO)

HP_SYSTEM_L2_MEM_UNIT2_REFRESH_DONE Represents the refresh status of L2MEM unit2 ECC bit. (RO)

HP_SYSTEM_L2_MEM_UNIT3_REFRESH_DONE Represents the refresh status of L2MEM unit3 ECC bit. (RO)
