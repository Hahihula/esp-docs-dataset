Register 39.91. H264_DMA_IN_RO_PD_CONF_CHO_REG (0x0548)

H264_DMA_IN_RO_RAM_CLK_FO_CHO   Configures whether to force enable clock gating for DMA RX reorder RAM.

0: A clock gating will be used when accessing the RAM in DMA
1: Force to open the clock and bypass the clock gating when accessing the RAM in DMA (R/W)
