Register 43.15. SPI_DIN_NUM_REG (0x0028)

Continued from the previous page...

SPI_DIN4_NUM Configures the delays to input signal SPI2D4 based on the setting of SPI_DIN4_MODE.
- 0: Delayed by 1 clock cycle
- 1: Delayed by 2 clock cycles
- 2: Delayed by 3 clock cycles
- 3: Delayed by 4 clock cycles

Can be configured in CONF state.
(R/W)
