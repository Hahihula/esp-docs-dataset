Register 36.76. ISP_BLC_CTRL0_REG (0x0194)

| Bit | Description                                                                 |
|-----|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 31  | (reserved)                                                                  |
| 3   | ISP_BLC_R3_STRETCH                                                          |
| 2   | ISP_BLC_R2_STRETCH                                                          |
| 1   | ISP_BLC_R1_STRETCH                                                          |
| 0   | ISP_BLC_RO_STRETCH                                                          |

ISP_BLC_R3_STRETCH Configures whether to stretch the pixel range of channel 3.
- 0: Stretch
- 1: Not stretch
(R/W)

ISP_BLC_R2_STRETCH Configures whether to stretch the pixel range of channel 2.
- 0: Stretch
- 1: Not stretch
(R/W)

ISP_BLC_R1_STRETCH Configures whether to stretch the pixel range of channel 1.
- 0: Stretch
- 1: Not stretch
(R/W)

ISP_BLC_RO_STRETCH Configures whether to stretch the pixel range of channel 0.
- 0: Stretch
- 1: Not stretch
(R/W)
