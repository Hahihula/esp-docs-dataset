```markdown
Register 17.13. RTC_WDT_INT_CLR_REG (0x0030)

RTC_WDT_SWDT_INT_CLR   Configure whether to clear the timeout interrupt signal sent by SWD to CPU.
    0: No
    1: Yes
        (WT)

RTC_WDT_RWDT_INT_CLR   Configure whether to clear the timeout interrupt signal sent by RWDT to CPU.
    0: No
    1: Yes
        (WT)
```
