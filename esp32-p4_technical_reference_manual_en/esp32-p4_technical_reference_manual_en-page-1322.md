Register 20.102. LP_SYSTEM_LP_CORE_IBUS_TIMEOUT_REG (0x01B4)

LP_SYSTEM_LP_CORE_IBUS_TIMEOUT_EN Configures whether or not to enable timeout protection on LP CPU IBUS.
O: Disable
1: Enable
(R/W)

LP_SYSTEM_LP_CORE_IBUS_TIMEOUT_THRES Configures LP_CORE_IBUS_TIMEOUT_INT threshold. (R/W)

Register 20.103. LP_SYSTEM_LP_CORE_DBUS_TIMEOUT_REG (0x01B8)

LP_SYSTEM_LP_CORE_DBUS_TIMEOUT_EN Configures whether or not to enable timeout protection on LP CPU DBUS.
O: Disable
1: Enable
(R/W)

LP_SYSTEM_LP_CORE_DBUS_TIMEOUT_THRES Configures LP_CORE_DBUS_TIMEOUT_INT threshold. (R/W)
