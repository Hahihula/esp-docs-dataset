Register 18.3. RTC_TIMER_TAR1_LOW_REG (0x0008)

RTC_TIMER_MAIN_TIMER_TAR_LOW1   Configure the low 32 bits of the target time 1. (R/W)


Register 18.4. RTC_TIMER_TAR1_HIGH_REG (0x000C)

RTC_TIMER_MAIN_TIMER_TAR_HIGH1   Configure the high 16 bits of the target time 1. (R/W)
RTC_TIMER_MAIN_TIMER_TAR_EN1     Configure to enable the target time 1 of the RTC Timer. (WO)
