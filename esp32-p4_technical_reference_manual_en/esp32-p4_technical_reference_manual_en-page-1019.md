Register 14.19. PMU_HP_SLEEP_HP_REGULATORO_REG (0x0090)

PMU_HP_SLEEP_HP_REGULATOR_DBIAS Configures whether to enable the HP sys regulator in HP_SLEEP state.
- O: Disable
- 1: Enable
(R/W)

PMU_HP_SLEEP_HP_REGULATOR_VO1_XPD Configures whether to enable VO1 regulator in HP_SLEEP state.
- O: Disable
- 1: Enable
(R/W)

PMU_HP_SLEEP_HP_REGULATOR_DBIAS Regulates the voltage of the HP sys regulator in HP_SLEEP state. The higher the value, the higher the voltage. (R/W)

Register 14.20. PMU_HP_SLEEP_XTAL_REG (0x0098)

PMU_HP_SLEEP_XPD_XTAL Configures whether to enable XTAL_CLK analog source in HP_SLEEP state.
- O: Disable
- 1: Enable
(R/W)
