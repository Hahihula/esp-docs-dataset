H264_A_ROI_REGIONO_X Configures the horizontal start MBs of ROI O in Video sequence A. (R/W)

H264_A_ROI_REGIONO_Y Configures the vertical start MBs of ROI O in Video sequence A. (R/W)

H264_A_ROI_REGIONO_X_LEN Configures the number of MBs in horizontal direction of the ROI O in Video sequence A. (R/W)

H264_A_ROI_REGIONO_Y_LEN Configures the number of MBs in vertical direction of the ROI O in Video sequence A. (R/W)

H264_A_ROI_REGIONO_EN Configures whether to enable Video sequence A ROI O.
0: Disable ROI
1: Enable ROI
(R/W)
