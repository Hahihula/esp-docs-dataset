## 21.7.3 Other Registers

Register 21.26. ASSIST_DEBUG_CORE_O_MONTR_ENA_REG (0x0000)

ASSIST_DEBUG_CORE_O_AREA_DRAMO_O_RD_ENA Configures whether to monitor read operations in HP CPUO region O by the HP CPUO Data bus.
- O: Not monitor
- 1: Monitor
(R/W)

ASSIST_DEBUG_CORE_O_AREA_DRAMO_O_WR_ENA Configures whether to monitor write operations in HP CPUO region O by the HP CPUO Data bus.
- O: Not monitor
- 1: Monitor
(R/W)

ASSIST_DEBUG_CORE_O_AREA_DRAMO_1_RD_ENA Configures whether to monitor read operations in HP CPUO region 1 by the HP CPUO Data bus.
- O: Not monitor
- 1: Monitor
(R/W)

ASSIST_DEBUG_CORE_O_AREA_DRAMO_1_WR_ENA Configures whether to monitor write operations in HP CPUO region 1 by the HP CPUO Data bus.
- O: Not monitor
- 1: Monitor
(R/W)

ASSIST_DEBUG_CORE_O_AREA_PIF_O_RD_ENA Configures whether to monitor read operations in region O by the Peripheral bus.
- O: Not monitor
- 1: Monitor
(R/W)
