H264_A_ROI_REGION5_X Configures the horizontal start MBs of ROI 5 video sequence A. (R/W)

H264_A_ROI_REGION5_Y Configures the vertical start MBs of ROI 5 video sequence A. (R/W)

H264_A_ROI_REGION5_X_LEN Configures the number of MBs in horizontal direction of the ROI 5 video sequence A. (R/W)

H264_A_ROI_REGION5_Y_LEN Configures the number of MBs in vertical direction of the ROI 5 in video sequence A. (R/W)

H264_A_ROI_REGION5_EN Configures whether to enable Video sequence A ROI 5.
0: Disable ROI
1: Enable ROI
