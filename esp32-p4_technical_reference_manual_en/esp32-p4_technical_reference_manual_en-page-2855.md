MCPWM_GENn_B_DTO Configures action on PWMn B triggered by event_t0 when timer decreasing.
O: No change
1: Low
2: High
3: Toggle
(R/W)

MCPWM_GENn_B_DT1 Configures action on PWMn B triggered by event_t1 when timer decreasing.
O: No change
1: Low
2: High
3: Toggle
(R/W)
