Chapter 14 Low-Power Management

Register 14.5. PMU_HP_ACTIVE_BIAS_REG (0x0018)

PMU_HP_ACTIVE_DCM_VSET    Configures whether to enable DCDC status in HP_ACTIVE state.
                         O: Disable
                         1: Enable
                         2, 3: Reserved
