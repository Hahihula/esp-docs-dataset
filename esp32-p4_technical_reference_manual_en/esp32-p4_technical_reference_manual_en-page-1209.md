Register 19.70. PMS_LP2HP_PERI_PMS_CLK_EN_REG (0x0004)

PMS_LP2HP_PERI_PMS_CLK_EN Configures whether to keep the clock always on.
O: Enable automatic clock gating.
1: Keep the clock always on.
