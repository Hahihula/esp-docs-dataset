**Subtitle:**
3.1 Pin Layout

**Body Text:**
The pin diagram below shows the approximate location of pins on the module. For the actual diagram drawn to scale, please refer to Chapter 10.1 Module Dimensions.

**Diagram Description (Figure Caption):**
Figure 3-1. ESP32-C5-WROOM-1 Layout (Top View)

**Diagram Labels:**
- Keepout Zone
- GND - Ground pins labeled from IO14 to IO28 and also at positions 1, 27.
- EN pin marked as position 3 on the left side of the diagram.
- TX0 positioned near label number 25 in the middle right section under "IO26".
- RX0 located next to TX0 labeled with a dashed box indicating it is not connected (NC).
- IO pins numbered from top-left corner starting at positions like IO1, IO3 up until bottom-right position marked as IO28.
- Additional labels include 29 GND and NC/IO15.
