Register 7.56. PCR_IOMUX_CLK_CONF_REG (0x00EC)

PCR_IOMUX_FUNC_CLK_SEL Configures the clock source of IO MUX.
- 0: XTAL_CLK
- 1: RC_FAST_CLK
- 2: PLL_F48M_CLK
- 3: No clock source
(R/W)

PCR_IOMUX_FUNC_CLK_EN Configures whether or not to enable IO MUX functional clock.
- 0: Not enable
- 1: Enable
(R/W)
