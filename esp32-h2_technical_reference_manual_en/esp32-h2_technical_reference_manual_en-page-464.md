Timer Group Architecture\n\nFigure 13.3-1 is a diagram of timer TO in a timer group. TO contains a 16-bit integer divider as a prescaler, a timer-based counter and a comparator for alarm generation.\n\n### 13.3.1 16-bit Prescaler and Clock Selection\n\nTake the TO in TIMGO as an example:\n\n- The timer can select its clock source by setting the `PCR_TGO_TIMER_CLK_SEL` field of the `PCR_TIMERGROUP0_TIMER_CLK_CONF_REG` register. When the field is 0, XTAL_CLK is selected; when the field is 1, RC_FAST_CLK is selected and when the field is 2, PLL_F48M_CLK is selected.\n- The selected clock can be switched on by setting `PCR_TGO_TIMER_CLK_EN` field of the `PCR_TIMERGROUP0_TIMER_CLK_CONF_REG` register to 1 and switched off by setting it to 0. The clock is then divided by a 16-bit prescaler to generate the time-base counter clock (TB_CLK) used by the time-base counter. The divisor of the prescaler can be configured through the `TIMGO_TO_DIVIDER` field. `TIMGO_TO_DIVIDER` field can be configured as 0 ~ 65535 for a divisor range of 2 ~ 65536. To be more specific, when `TIMGO_TO_DIVIDER` is configured as:\n\n  - 0: the divisor is 65536\n  - 1: the divisor is 2\n  - 2: the divisor is also 2\n  - 3 ~ 65535: the divisor is 3 ~ 65535\n\nTo modify the 16-bit prescaler, please first configure the `TIMGO_TO_DIVIDER` field, and then set `TIMGO_TO_DIVCNT_RST` to 1. Meanwhile, the timer must be disabled (i.e., `TIMGO_TO_EN` should be cleared). Otherwise, the result can be unpredictable.\n```
