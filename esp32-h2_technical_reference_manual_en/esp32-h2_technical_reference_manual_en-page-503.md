Chapter 14 Watchdog Timers (WDT)

Register 14.13. RTC_WDT_INT_CLR_REG (0x0034)

RTC_WDT_SWD_INT_CLR   Configure whether to clear the timeout interrupt signal sent by SWD to CPU.
O: No
1: Yes
(WT)

RTC_WDT_INT_CLR       Configure whether to clear the timeout interrupt signal sent by RWDT to CPU.
O: No
1: Yes
(WT)

Register 14.14. RTC_WDT_DATE_REG (0x03FC)

RTC_WDT_DATE          Version control register (R/W)
