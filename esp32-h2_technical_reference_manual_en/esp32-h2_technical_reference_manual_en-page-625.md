```markdown
Chapter 21 HMAC Accelerator (HMAC)

Register 21.7 HMAC_SET_INVALIDATE_JTAG_REG (0x0060)

HMAC_SET_INVALIDATE_JTAG Configures whether to clear calculation results when re-enabling JTAG in downstream mode.
- O: Not clear
- 1: Clear calculation results
(WO)
```

```markdown
Register 21.8 HMAC_SET_INVALIDATE_DS_REG (0x0064)

HMAC_SET_INVALIDATE_DS Configures whether to clear calculation results of the DSA module in downstream mode.
- O: Not clear
- 1: Clear calculation results
(WO)
```
