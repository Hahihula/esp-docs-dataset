Chapter 7 Reset and Clock

Register 7.34. PCR_USB_SERIAL_JTAG_CONF_REG (0x008C)

PCR_USB_SERIAL_JTAG_CLK_EN   Configures whether or not to enable USB Serial/JTAG clock.
O: Not enable
1: Enable
(R/W)

PCR_USB_SERIAL_JTAG_RST_EN    Configures whether or not to reset USB Serial/JTAG.
O: Not reset
1: Reset
(R/W)

PCR_USB_SERIAL_JTAG_READY     Represents whether or not USB Serial/JTAG is released from re-
set.
O: Not released
1: Released
(RO)
