Register 3.37. GDMA_OUT_PERI_SEL_CHn_REG (n: 0-2) (0x0100+0xC0*n)

GDMA_PERI_OUT_SEL_CHn   Configures the peripheral connected to TX channel n.

0: SPI2
1: Dummy-1
2: UHCI
3: I2S
4: Dummy-4
5: Dummy-5
6: AES
7: SHA
8: ADC
9: Parallel IO
10 ~ 15: Dummy-10 ~ 15
16 ~ 63: Invalid.
(R/W)
